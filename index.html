1. Learn the 4 basic sounds

B BassHit the centre with a relaxed palm. Deep sound.
T ToneUse fingers near the edge. Clear, open sound.
S SlapLight, quick fingers near the edge. Crisp sound.
R RestKeep the pulse moving but do not hit.
Beginner rule: relax your hands. Your first goal is a steady pulse, not speed.

Safety & technique

Keep wrists and fingers relaxed. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or unusual discomfort. Speed comes after control.
